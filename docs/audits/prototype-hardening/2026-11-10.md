## Invariant and repairs

A rendition change or reload of the same clip keeps the viewer's moment and
play state. Selection lives in one `choiceRef` instead of four refs and two
states. Switches, manual retries and automatic recovery share one
`continuity()` snapshot. A failure before the new source's metadata keeps the
earlier moment. Playback routes redirect to short-lived grants, so a network
error, or a refusal from a source that already played, reloads in place at
most `MAX_AUTO_RECOVERIES` times. After that, the error card offers Retry
(same quality and moment) and a lower rendition. A preparation request that
resolves after the viewer moved to another clip is ignored.

Quality is an explicit menu (`QualityMenu`) in the viewer, studio and embedded
players. Keys handled inside it do not reach viewer shortcuts. The transport
grid is shared by studio and phone and grows columns for optional controls.
The analysis header shows counts only once analysis data exists.
